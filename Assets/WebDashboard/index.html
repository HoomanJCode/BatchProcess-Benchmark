Gravity calculation samples show average time per physics step. If Unity runs multiple physics steps in one rendered frame, their summed marker time is divided by the marker call count. Coroutine chunk times are summed into one complete gravity step across frames. Frame times separately show overall responsiveness and include Unity physics and Editor overhead. For a fair CPU comparison, keep the ball count and machine the same, and use a standalone Development Build for final numbers.
